**Trady Perch Hero Experience Bible · Appendices**
*First-canonical-draft.*

**Purpose.** Three indexes: every numbered principle with its chapter; every inherited claim's trace to its source document; and a consolidated ledger of what remains unwritten.

## 4. STATUS

**This appendix's own limitation.** §2's index was assembled by tracing citations chapter by chapter. Where a sibling document was read via this repository's synthesis documents rather than in full — which applies to parts of the Design System Bible and to nearly all of the Motion Bible and UX Blueprint, both of which exist primarily as architecture documents with unwritten chapters — the citation reflects what those syntheses record. Any misattribution should be corrected per Fx-5, alongside the original rather than in place of it.

**Maintenance.** §1 must be updated whenever a principle is added, removed, or renumbered — it is the instrument Chapter 32's step 4 depends on. §3.1 shrinks as sibling chapters are written; §3.2 as real content arrives; §3.3 as claims are validated or refuted. A version in which none of the three has changed is a signal that the Bible is not being used.
